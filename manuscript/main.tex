\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Part~I gives two inequivalent readings of the same real six-dimensional space $\R^{3,3}$, as a displacement and as an electromagnetic field
strength, with different Lorentz actions. This Part develops the field dynamics of the interior shift, a Clifford transport between the two
readings, and a one-family construction. Replacing the constant shift by $G\circ\curl$ recovers Maxwell evolution in the zero-current sector
when Gauss's laws hold, and the six-component momentum becomes the pair of spatial currents. On a constant-rate background a massive two-form
sector has positive canonical energy; coupled to a connection, its tree amplitudes grow with energy, and an $\so3$ Yang--Mills--Higgs
completion cancels the leading growth in the fixed-angle tree amplitudes examined. The transport map, in the split Clifford algebra
$\Cl(3n,3n)$ and for a fixed Lorentz four-plane, replaces the clock generator by the volume element of the full algebra. At $n=1$ the
transported temporal algebra is the entire commutant of the Lorentz algebra, and left multiplication by its transported volume implements
duality on field-strength bivectors; at general $n$, with the spectator Lorentz action, the commutant is $\Cl(3n,3n-3)$ and the transported
rotations form $\so{3n}$. At $n=2$, gauging these rotations together with the two weak factors supplied by the spatial Clifford algebra gives
one chiral Pati--Salam family, with a right-handed neutrino, on the full spinor module. For the chosen Standard-Model embedding the invariant
mass kernels form one complex Majorana line; a nonzero value on it, together with the bidoublet prescribed by the transported volume, leaves
$\su3\oplus\uu1_Q$ unbroken. A three-parameter choice of neutral Dirac kernels gives a linear relation among four signed Dirac entries. At the
specified two-stage background, an allowed invariant quartic has a nonzero gradient transverse to this choice, computed in closed form, so the
imposed symmetries do not enforce the relation. Vacuum alignment, mass scales and the number of light families remain open.

\medskip\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} split-signature Clifford algebras; curl realisation of Maxwell evolution; massive two-form fields; tree
unitarity; Clifford transport; commutants of Lorentz algebras; Pati--Salam models; electroweak symmetry breaking; fermion mass relations;
vacuum alignment; computer-assisted algebra.
\end{abstract}
